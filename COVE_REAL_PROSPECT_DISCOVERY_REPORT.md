## A. Research Universe & Screening Criteria

* **Universe Investigated**: Over 45 Indonesian private construction contractors screened across West Java (Bandung Raya), DKI Jakarta / Jabodetabek, East Java (Surabaya/Sidoarjo), Central Java (Semarang/Solo), and regional hubs (Balikpapan, Medan, Yogyakarta).
* **ICP Inclusion Criteria**:
  1. Private mid-market contractor (Annual revenue estimate $\text{Rp 20B – Rp 250B}$);
  2. Publicly verifiable active projects (commercial buildings, industrial warehouses, civil works, institutional facilities);
  3. Likely monthly progress billing (interim opname and BAP certification workflow);
  4. Moderate organization scale enabling direct access to the Managing Director, Commercial Director, or Head of QS.
* **Exclusion Criteria**:
  - Mega SOE / BUMN Tbk contractors (WIKA, PTPP, ADHI, Waskita) excluded for Pilot #1 to avoid heavy enterprise procurement latency.
  - Micro-builders, renovation shops, material suppliers, and pure architectural design firms excluded.
