# COVE — Real 20 Indonesian Contractor Prospect Research Dossier

> **Methodology**: Research conducted using publicly accessible and verifiable sources (official company websites, public project portfolios, construction industry directories, public business registries).  
> **Integrity Rule**: Every finding is categorized as **FACT** (traceable to source), **INFERENCE** (reasoned hypothesis), or **UNKNOWN** (unverified). Unknowns receive 0 points in the Research Fit Score.  
> **Output Classification**: All 20 companies are classified as **POTENTIAL FIT** (not qualified customers). Direct customer qualification occurs only through real founder discovery conversations.
