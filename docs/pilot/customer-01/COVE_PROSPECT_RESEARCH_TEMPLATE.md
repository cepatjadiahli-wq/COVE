# COVE — Prospect Operational Pre-Call Research Template

> **Purpose**: Standardized 1-page research dossier to prepare before reaching out or conducting a discovery call with a contractor prospect.  
> **Golden Rule**: Explicitly label every piece of information as **[FACT]**, **[INFERENCE]**, or **[UNKNOWN]** to prevent founder hallucination.

---

* **Key Decision Maker Contact**: `[FACT]` [Nama Direktur / Managing Director, Nomor WA, Email]
* **Key Operational Contact**: `[INFERENCE]` [Head of QS / Commercial Manager]

## 2. Operational Hypothesis (Before Discovery Call)

| Operational Dimension | Hypothesis & Initial Assessment | Fact vs Inference |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Progress Billing Relevance** | Uses monthly progress claims (MC-001, MC-002...) with opname and MK certification | `[INFERENCE]` |
| **Commercial / QS Presence** | Has dedicated Project QS who calculates volume and submits BAP to consultant | `[INFERENCE]` |
| **Finance Participation** | Has separate finance billing officer who generates invoices after BAP approval | `[INFERENCE]` |
| **Workflow Fragmentation** | Site engineers use WhatsApp to share opname photos; QS uses standalone Excel; Finance uses basic accounting software | `[INFERENCE]` |
| **Executive Visibility** | Managing Director asks for verbal updates during Monday meetings because there is no single live dashboard | `[INFERENCE]` |

## 3. Probable Commercial Pain & Cash-at-Risk Triggers

* **Primary Commercial Pain**:
  - `[INFERENCE]` Selisih perhitungan volume pembesian/fasade tertahan di konsultan MK $\rightarrow$ Uncertified Gap >Rp 500M.
  - `[INFERENCE]` Dokumen QC / BA uji tes material terlambat diserahkan tim lapangan $\rightarrow$ Menunda pengajuan MC.
  - `[UNKNOWN]` Berapa hari rata-rata keterlambatan pembayaran invoice klien setelah BAP disetujui.
* **Direct Evidence / Known Facts**:
  - `[FACT]` Perusahaan sedang mengerjakan proyek komersial 18 lantai di area TB Simatupang dengan konsultan MK independen.
  - `[FACT]` Direktur Utama adalah pengambil keputusan tunggal untuk adopsi software operasional.

---

## 4. Discovery Strategy & Key Custom Angle

* **Angle to Lead With**: Fokus pada selisih opname fisik vs BAP disetujui konsultan MK pada proyek komersial bertingkat.
* **Key Question to Open Discovery**:
  > *"Pak [Nama], di proyek gedung bertingkat yang sedang jalan saat ini, biasanya bagian mana yang paling sering memakan waktu saat rekonsiliasi opname volume dengan konsultan MK?"*
