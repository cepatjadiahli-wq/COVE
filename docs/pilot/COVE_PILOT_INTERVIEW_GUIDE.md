# COVE — Pilot Customer 20-Question Qualitative Interview Guide

> **Timing**: Conducted at Day 25–30 of the Pilot  
> **Duration**: 30–45 Minutes  
> **Rule**: Do **NOT** ask *"Fitur apa yang Anda inginkan?"*. Ask about past behavior, emotional friction, information trust, and commercial willingness to pay.

---

## Part 1: Baseline Workflow & Information Friction (Past vs Present)

1. **Before COVE, how did your team track monthly progress claims and billing status?**
   *(Listen for: Excel sheets per project, physical paper binders, WhatsApp group updates, manual recap meetings).*
2. **When a progress claim was delayed by MK or Client, where did you usually have to look to find the root cause?**
   *(Listen for: Asking QS verbally, searching through 200+ WhatsApp chat histories, checking physical minutes of meeting).*
3. **Which economic metric was historically the hardest to calculate or know accurately in real-time?**
   *(Listen for: Uncertified gap, pre-invoice exposure, retention balances, aging days in review).*
4. **During the 30-day pilot, what commercial or financial tasks did your team still have to do outside COVE in Excel?**
   *(Listen for: Detailed BOQ calculations, subcontractor deductions, daily progress logs).*
5. **What information did you still have to search for in WhatsApp groups?**
   *(Listen for: Document attachments, consultant signatures, photos of field opname).*

---

## Part 2: Trust, Accuracy & Economic Relevance

6. **Which specific numbers in COVE did your team trust without hesitation?**
   *(Listen for: Work performed, claimed value, certified value, invoice gross/net).*
7. **Were there any numbers or alerts in COVE that you questioned or found confusing at first?**
   *(Listen for: Retention deductions, cash-at-risk formula, SLA aging warnings).*
8. **Did COVE reveal any stuck value or delay that you or the directors were previously unaware of?**
   *(Listen for: Unclaimed gap after opname, MC stuck for >14 days in consultant review).*
9. **Which economic exposure identified during the pilot was most critical to your company's cash flow?**
   *(Listen for: Specific Rupiah amount, e.g. Rp 500M uncertified volume).*
10. **Did using COVE change any specific decision or action your team took with the client or consultant MK?**
    *(Listen for: Expedited technical meeting, escalated letter, earlier invoice issuance).*

---

## Part 3: Role Adoption & Operational Reality

11. **In your company, who benefited the most from using COVE during the past month?**
    *(Listen for: Commercial Manager, Senior QS, Managing Director, Finance Manager).*
12. **Who in the team rarely opened or used COVE, and why?**
    *(Listen for: Site Project Manager, junior engineers, administrative staff).*
13. **What was the biggest friction or obstacle that prevented your team from using COVE more frequently?**
    *(Listen for: Lack of mobile access on site, habit of opening Excel, forgetting password).*

---

## Part 4: Counterfactual / Disappearance Test

14. **If COVE were shut down tomorrow and you had to return 100% to your previous tools, what would you miss most?**
    *(Listen for: Immediate visual Money Pipeline, Cash-at-Risk alert, centralized blocker tracking).*
15. **If COVE were shut down, what would you NOT miss at all?**
    *(Listen for: Redundant data entry fields, confusing settings).*

---

## Part 5: Commercial Continuation & Willingness-to-Pay (WTP)

16. **Does your company want to continue using COVE after this 30-day pilot concludes?**
    *(Look for strong immediate affirmation vs polite hesitation).*
17. **How many active projects would you want to manage on COVE if you continue?**
    *(Listen for: 2–3 projects, all active company projects).*
18. **Who in your company has the formal authority to approve a software subscription for COVE?**
    *(Listen for: Direktur Utama / Managing Director, Direktur Keuangan, Direktur Operasional).*
19. **From which budget category would this subscription be paid?**
    *(Listen for: Biaya Operasional Kantor Pusat / Overhead, Biaya Tidak Langsung Proyek / Project Indirect Cost).*
20. **What is one critical improvement or condition that must be met before your company approves the purchase?**
    *(Listen for: Specific report format, mobile responsiveness, pricing model).*
