# COVE — Controlled Early Access Pilot Offer & Terms

> **Document Type**: Pilot Customer Agreement & Proposition Framework  
> **Target Audience**: Contractor Managing Directors & Commercial Directors  
> **Duration**: 30 Calendar Days

---

## 1. Product Proposition & Core Promise

> *"COVE membantu direksi dan tim komersial kontraktor mengetahui persis di mana nilai proyek tertahan sebelum menjadi kas, berapa Rupiah yang terdampak, apa penyebab kendalanya, siapa PIC-nya, dan tindakan nyata apa yang harus dilakukan minggu ini."*

### What COVE Guarantees:
- **100% Visibility of Project Value Flow**: Real-time Money Pipeline tracking from Work Performed $\rightarrow$ Opname $\rightarrow$ Claim Submission $\rightarrow$ MK Certification $\rightarrow$ Invoicing $\rightarrow$ Cash Receipt.
- **Immediate Detection of Cash-at-Risk**: Identification of unmeasured volume, uncertified claims, overdue receivables, and SLA bottlenecks.
- **Actionable Operational Accountability**: Clear assignment of blockers and actions to PICs with target resolution dates.

### What COVE Does NOT Promise:
- We do **not** promise or guarantee that project owners/clients will immediately pay invoices faster without client-side liquidity.
- We do **not** replace standard general accounting software (e.g. tax reporting, payroll, general ledger).

---

## 2. Pilot Scope & Inclusions

During the 30-day pilot, the participating contractor receives:

1. **Dedicated Tenant Account**: Secure, isolated multi-tenant organization in COVE with PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS).
2. **1 Active Pilot Project**: Full setup of one active commercial, infrastructure, or industrial project.
3. **Up to 5 User Licenses**:
   - 1 $\times$ Owner / Director (Portfolio & Executive Cash-at-Risk)
   - 1 $\times$ Commercial Manager / Lead QS (Claims & Recertification)
   - 1 $\times$ Project QS (Opname & Evidence Checklist)
   - 1 $\times$ Finance & Billing Manager (Invoicing & Cash Receipts)
   - 1 $\times$ Project Manager (Site Status & Field Blockers)
4. **Historical & Current Claims Import**: Assisted onboarding and mapping of 3 to 10 recent monthly progress claims (MC).
5. **Weekly Commercial Value Review (15–20 Mins)**: Structured weekly check-in with the COVE founder to review exposures and action progress.
6. **COVE Pilot Value Summary Document**: Formal executive report at Day 30 summarizing all monitored contract value, detected Cash-at-Risk, resolved actions, and verified management outcomes.

## 4. Contractor Commitments

To ensure a successful pilot, the contractor agrees to:
- Assign 1 designated Operational Point of Contact (Commercial Manager or Senior QS).
- Provide historical data for 1 active project (Contract value, recent 3–10 MC claim numbers, opname values, and invoice statuses).
- Attend four 15-minute weekly sync sessions (via Zoom or in-person).
- Provide honest, transparent operational feedback on workflow usability and missing information.
